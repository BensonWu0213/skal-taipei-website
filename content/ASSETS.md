### Official Skål International logo (vector) — SOLVED
Source: the public **Skål International Brand Kit** (Google Drive folder linked from the 2025 press kit, https://skal.org/press-kit).
The CMYK PDF in that kit is pure vector (Illustrator export, no fonts, no bitmaps), so we converted it to SVG — **no AI tracing needed**.
Official colours (press kit p.14): dark blue `#314691`, light blue `#65A8DE`, shield gradient 90° with the break at ~62 %, text grey `#59595B`.

| File | What it is | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `skal-international-logos-official-cmyk.pdf` | The untouched official vector file (3 pages: colour / white text + colour shield / all white). | Archive, hand to printers. |
| `skal-international-logo-official.png` (3154×1838), `…-official-white.png`, `…-official-square-400.png` | The untouched official PNGs from the kit. | og:image, favicon source, anywhere a PNG is simpler. |
| `skal-international-logo.svg` (+ `.png` 1600 px) | Clean SVG of the colour logo, gradient rebuilt as a real `<linearGradient>` (the PDF→SVG export had rasterised it). | Global logo where needed (e.g. "member of" footer). |

## `public/images/feed/` — auto-generated, do not edit by hand

## `content/facebook-feed.json` + `public/feed.xml`
- JSON: `{ source, generatedAt, count, posts: [{ id, date, title, text, url, images[], hasText }] }`,
  newest first, merged with previous runs so nothing is lost when FB stops showing old posts.
- `public/feed.xml`: RSS 2.0 of the same posts (lets members subscribe / lets other tools consume it).

## Key facts for site copy (from posts)
- Founded 1970 (Page slug "Since1970"). Global Skål founded 1934; ~12,000 members, 100+ countries.
- Contact: skalinternationaltaipei@gmail.com. Registration via Google Forms per event.
- Monthly General Meetings (lunch, usually a Wednesday noon at a member hotel/restaurant: S-Aura Hotel, WINE-derful, Radium Kagaya, Howard Plaza, Grand Hotel…). Free for members, guest fee applies, business casual.
- Signature programmes: "Doing Business Among Friends", World Tourism Day, Skål Taipei Toastmasters (chartered club), Young Skål, Taiwan–Japan twinning (Taipei/Taichung/Kaohsiung ↔ Tokyo/Osaka/Nagoya), twinning with Skål Cusco, sustainability (reforestation, Sustainable Tourism Awards).
- People: President Windy Yang; Club Founder Edward Lo; Charter (Toastmasters) President Mayumi Hu; Membership Director Doi Yasunori (土井康敬); Past Presidents Steven H. Sasaya (2017–18), Hiro Liao (†2026-09-04).
- Diplomatic ties: ambassadors of Saint Lucia, Belize, Saint Vincent & the Grenadines are members/guests.
