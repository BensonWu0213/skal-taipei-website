# Skal International Taipei — Website Design Brief

## Organization Overview

**Name**: Skal International Taipei  
**Founded**: 1970 (since 1970)  
**Industry**: Tourism, Hospitality & Travel  
**Type**: Nonprofit / Professional Organization  
**Headcount**: ~3 people (distributed across Taiwan and India)  
**Parent Organization**: Skal International (founded 1934, headquartered globally)  

---

## Mission & Values

### What Skal Does
Skal International is a **professional organization uniting tourism leaders** from all sectors of the travel & hospitality industry. It's the **only international group** that brings together all branches of travel/tourism (hotels, airlines, tour operators, travel agents, etc.).

**Key Actions**:
- Connects tourism professionals for collaboration and growth
- Promotes global tourism and sustainable practices
- Facilitates knowledge-sharing & networking across the industry
- Hosts events, forums, and conferences (e.g., World Tourism Day Forum)
- Supports Taiwan Tourism Bureau initiatives

### Core Values
- **Sustainability**: Promoting sustainable tourism practices
- **Collaboration**: Uniting all tourism sectors
- **Leadership**: Industry managers and executives
- **Global Connection**: Spanning 100+ countries with 12,000+ members
- **Friendship & Professionalism**: Building meaningful relationships across tourism

---

## Website Goals

### Primary Objectives
1. **Attract & engage tourism professionals** in Taiwan to join Skal

### Key Audiences
- Tourism industry professionals (hotels, airlines, tour operators, travel agencies, F&B)
- Executives and managers in hospitality/travel
- Potential members interested in networking
- International partners / other Skal chapters
- Media, government (Taiwan Tourism Bureau, Ministry of Transportation)

## Brand Identity

### Color Palette
**Primary Colors**:
- **Deep Navy Blue**: `#003366` or `#1a3a52` → Trust, professionalism, global reach
- **Gold/Warm Accent**: `#D4A574` or `#C9975A` → Warmth, hospitality, luxury
- **Cream/Off-White**: `#F5F1ED` or `#FAFBF8` → Clean, modern, accessible

**Secondary Colors**:
- **Light Gray**: `#E8E8E8` → Borders, dividers, subtle backgrounds
- **Green**: `#2D6A4F` or `#5A8C6E` → Sustainability (optional, for eco-messaging)

**Rationale**: 
- Navy conveys global professionalism & trust
- Gold adds warmth, aligns with hospitality/luxury (Skal's industry)
- Cream keeps it modern and uncluttered

### Typography
**Heading Font**: Serif or strong sans-serif (e.g., Georgia, Playfair Display, Montserrat Bold)
- Conveys leadership, authority, established heritage (since 1970)

**Body Font**: Clean sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Open Sans, Lato)
- Modern, readable, accessible for long-form content

**Tone of Voice**:
- Professional yet approachable
- Confident but not arrogant
- Inclusive and welcoming
- Industry-forward (mentioning sustainability, innovation)

## Design Style & Aesthetic

### Overall Feel
- **"Sleek and Modern"** with **professional gravitas**
- Clean layouts, ample whitespace
- Modern color palette (Navy + Gold + Cream)
- High-quality photography (professional networking, industry events)
- Subtle animations (hover effects, smooth transitions)
- Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

### Key Visual Elements
1. **Photography**: Diverse, professional, inclusive images of people networking, tourism settings
2. **Icons**: Simple, modern icons for benefits/features
3. **Patterns/Textures**: Subtle diagonal lines or modern geometric shapes (optional)
4. **Spacing**: Generous margins, breathing room
5. **Imagery Style**: Modern, candid, warm (not corporate/stiff)

### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliant
- Sufficient color contrast (Navy on Cream passes)
- Clear hierarchy, readable font sizes
- Alt text for all images
- Mobile-first responsive design
